# Implementation Report: Pitch Document

## Summary

Created a comprehensive 3-page pitch document for the SatTrack (Kessler) platform tailored for VCs and angel investors in the local community.

## Deliverables

- **Markdown**: `pitch-deck.md` (source format)
- **Word Document**: `pitch-deck.docx` (editable format, 15KB)

## Document Structure

### Page 1: The Opportunity
- **Problem**: Highlighted the crowded orbital space and limitations of current US-dominated, closed-source solutions
- **Solution**: Positioned SatTrack as an EU-hosted, open-source satellite tracking platform
- **Market Opportunity**: Defined TAM (€2.4B), SAM (€450M), and SOM (€12M in Year 3)

### Page 2: Product & Competitive Advantage
- **Product Architecture**: Described the FastAPI backend and React frontend with EU hosting
- **Competitive Advantage**: Emphasized EU data sovereignty, open-source approach, AI-based development velocity, and multi-source integration
- **Business Model**: Outlined freemium SaaS, API licensing, government contracts, and data enrichment services with revenue targets

### Page 3: Traction, Team & The Ask
- **Current Traction**: Working MVP with beta users
- **Team**: Solo technical founder with post-funding hiring plan
- **The Ask**: €200K-€300K seed funding for 12-month runway
- **Use of Funds**: Breakdown of product development (60%), go-to-market (20%), infrastructure (15%), and operating costs (5%)
- **Vision & Exit Strategy**: 12-24 month milestones and strategic acquisition targets (Airbus, Thales, OHB SE, US space-tech companies)

## Key Value Propositions

1. **EU Data Sovereignty**: All-EU hosting addressing GDPR and regulatory requirements
2. **Open Source**: Community-driven, transparent development
3. **AI Development Velocity**: 5-10x faster feature delivery
4. **Multi-source Integration**: Unified platform for UNOOSA, CelesTrak, and Space-Track data
